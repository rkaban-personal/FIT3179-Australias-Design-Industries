The supplied research reports identified useful leads. They were not treated as extracted datasets. Their starter-package links did not provide downloadable attachments in this workspace, so the reusable files were rebuilt from official sources. This summary records what was checked and what remains outside the evidence.

Corrections to the research blueprint

  • The JSA profiles describe the 2021 Census experimentally recoded to OSCA 2024, not employment in 2026.
  • Industrial design services fall within ANZSIC 6923, which also includes broader engineering consulting. Neither that entire class nor all computer-system services can be labelled design studios.
  • UI/UX correspondence depends on the older ANZSCO edition. The displayed 2022 edition maps User Experience Designer 261113 to OSCA 242132; it is not a universal ICT Business Analyst link.
  • The proposed design-specific education series could not be extracted from the downloaded pivot. The broader substitute is labelled as context.
  • Applications, registrations and certifications are separate administrative events. A same-year funnel or success rate would be unsupported.
  • At the audit date, detailed 2026 business-class data were scheduled for December. The maps consistently pair 2025 numerators and denominators.
  • The first web-generalised state file contained malformed tiny rings and failed visual inspection. It was replaced. A successful download alone did not establish valid geography.

How to check the story

Every chart links to its CSV, readable JSON specification and equivalent HTML table. Original workbooks and PDFs are retained separately from public extracts, with source hashes and row or page references in the local project audit folder. Source research, extracted data and implemented graphics are distinct layers.

The portfolio contains 13 figures and ten distinct advanced idiom candidates: choropleth, proportional-symbol map, filing-flow map, mosaic, waterfall, box plot, parallel coordinates, treemap, indexed heatmap and correspondence network. Repeated marks or series inside a chart are not counted as separate charts. This rationale does not guarantee a marking outcome.

Public data remain below 1 MB. The site uses local fonts and pinned chart libraries. Raw sources, private assignment documents and the student ID are excluded from the public package. An AI-assisted illustration introduces the narrative; it is not evidence or a submission sketch.

What the evidence cannot answer

Complete flows for all 58 reported IP origins, narrow-field design enrolments, comparable current economic estimates, sub-state designer counts and quantitative occupation-transition flows remain unavailable in this project. No invented values fill those gaps.

Text summaries, labels, line styles, keyboard controls and semantic tables support different ways of reading. These measures do not establish complete accessibility conformance or replace testing with assistive-technology users.
